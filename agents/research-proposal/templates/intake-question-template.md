# Design-critical clarification

Use only when the current idea cannot support a defensible design. Ask a maximum of three questions.

1. **[Decisive unknown]** Which option best matches your study? (Recommended: [option])
2. **[Feasibility unknown]** What access do you have to [participants/data/time]? (Recommended: [workable option])
3. **[Format or ethical unknown]** [Short, specific question] (Recommended: [safe/default option])

Proceeding assumption if no answer is available: **[clearly bounded assumption]**.
